# Commercial terms

## PB-PAY-001: Payment terms (we sell)
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, supply, vendor, freelance
role: vendor
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Invoices payable within 30 days of invoice date. Disputes must be raised in writing within 15 days, stating the reason. The undisputed part is still paid on time. Interest on late payment at the statutory rate.
**Fallback:** Up to 45 days. Payment linked to acceptance only for milestone payments, with deemed acceptance after 10 business days of silence.
**Walk-away:** More than 60 days, or payment conditional on the customer's "satisfaction" with no objective criteria.
**Red flags:** "pay-when-paid"; payment only "upon acceptance" with no deemed-acceptance mechanism; set-off of any amounts the customer claims; no late-payment remedy.
**Preferred language:** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Customer shall notify any good-faith dispute in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

## PB-PAY-002: Payment terms (we buy)
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, supply, vendor
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** 30–45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Right to withhold disputed amounts in good faith. No payment in advance for more than one year.
**Fallback:** Annual payment in advance for subscriptions, with a pro-rata refund if the agreement ends for the supplier's breach.
**Walk-away:** Multi-year prepayment with no refund right.
**Red flags:** Fees "non-refundable in all circumstances"; interest above statutory rates; suspension for any late payment without notice.
**Preferred language:** Customer shall pay each correct and undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## PB-PRICE-001: Price increases
applies_to: msa, saas, services, supply, vendor, sow
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Fixed prices for the initial term. After that, at most one increase a year, on at least 90 days' written notice, capped at CPI or 5% (whichever is lower).
**Fallback:** Cap at CPI + 3%, with a right to terminate without penalty if we reject the increase.
**Walk-away:** Unilateral increases "at any time" with no cap and no exit right.
**Red flags:** "Supplier may revise its fees from time to time"; prices "as set out on Supplier's website"; increases that take effect on renewal without advance notice.
**Preferred language:** Supplier may increase the Fees no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least ninety (90) days' written notice, by no more than the lower of five percent (5%) and the increase in CPI over the previous twelve (12) months.

## PB-PRICE-002: Price increases (we sell)
applies_to: msa, saas, services, supply, vendor
role: vendor
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Right to increase fees once a year on 60 days' notice, in line with our list prices.
**Fallback:** Cap at CPI + 5% for existing customers.
**Walk-away:** Prices fixed for more than 3 years with no indexation.
**Red flags:** Most-favoured-customer pricing; fixed prices for any renewal term.
**Preferred language:** Supplier may adjust the Fees with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date on at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

## PB-RENEW-001: Automatic renewal and notice
applies_to: msa, saas, services, supply, vendor
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** No auto-renewal, or renewal for 12-month terms at most, with a non-renewal notice period of 30–60 days. The supplier must remind us at least 30 days before the notice deadline.
**Fallback:** 90 days' notice if renewal terms are 12 months and the prices for the renewal term are fixed in advance.
**Walk-away:** Renewal for the full original multi-year term, or notice periods over 90 days with no reminder.
**Red flags:** "renews for successive periods equal to the Initial Term"; notice "at least 120 days"; renewal at "then-current list prices".
**Preferred language:** This Agreement shall renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## PB-TERM-001: Termination for convenience
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, supply, vendor
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** For services, we can terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice, paying for work done to date. For subscriptions, at the end of each annual period.
**Fallback:** 60–90 days' notice, or a reasonable wind-down fee covering committed costs.
**Walk-away:** Locked in for more than 3 years with no exit except the supplier's material breach.
**Red flags:** Only the supplier can terminate for convenience; all remaining fees for the full term become due on termination.
**Preferred language:** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice and shall pay for Services properly performed up to the termination date.

## PB-TERM-002: Termination for breach and insolvency
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, dpa, supply, vendor, freelance, reseller, partnership, licence, license
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice, and on the other party's insolvency. The rights are mutual.
**Fallback:** A shorter cure period (10 days) for non-payment only.
**Walk-away:** Only one party has termination rights, or there is no cure period for the other side's benefit.
**Red flags:** Termination "for any breach, however minor"; immediate termination without notice; termination rights for one side only.
**Preferred language:** Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which (if remediable) is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

## PB-SUSP-001: Suspension of service
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** The supplier may suspend only for undisputed amounts over 30 days overdue, after 10 days' written warning, or for a genuine security emergency, limited to what is necessary and lifted promptly.
**Fallback:** Suspension after 15 days overdue with 7 days' warning.
**Walk-away:** Suspension at the supplier's discretion without notice.
**Red flags:** "Supplier may suspend the Services at any time"; suspension that also blocks access to our data.
**Preferred language:** Supplier may suspend the Services only if undisputed fees are more than thirty (30) days overdue and Customer has not paid within ten (10) days of a written notice warning of suspension.

## PB-PREC-001: Order of precedence and entire agreement
applies_to: msa, saas, services, sow, supply, vendor, dpa
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Negotiated documents (signed agreement, SOW, DPA) override standard terms and online policies. Anything promised in our order form or proposal that matters is written into the contract.
**Fallback:** The supplier's terms prevail, except the DPA and the negotiated changes.
**Walk-away:** Online terms that can change override the signed agreement.
**Red flags:** "In the event of conflict, the Terms of Service shall prevail"; an entire-agreement clause wiping out features or service levels sales promised in writing; purchase-order terms overriding the agreement.
**Preferred language:** In the event of any conflict, the following order of precedence applies: (a) the Data Processing Agreement; (b) this Agreement; (c) the relevant Order Form or Statement of Work; (d) any other document referred to in this Agreement.

## PB-AMEND-001: Unilateral changes and online terms
applies_to: msa, saas, services, supply, vendor, dpa
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Changes need a written amendment signed by both parties. Policies referred to by URL are fixed as of signature, or changes cannot materially reduce our rights.
**Fallback:** The supplier may update policies on 30 days' notice, and we may terminate with a pro-rata refund if a change is materially adverse.
**Walk-away:** The supplier can change any term at any time just by posting it online.
**Red flags:** "as updated from time to time"; "available at [URL]"; "continued use constitutes acceptance".
**Preferred language:** No variation of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties. Supplier shall not change any policy referred to in this Agreement in a way that materially reduces Customer's rights or the functionality, security or service levels of the Services.
