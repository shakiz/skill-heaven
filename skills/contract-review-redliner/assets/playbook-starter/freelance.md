# Freelancers and small agencies (selling services)

## PB-FL-001: Acceptance and payment trigger
applies_to: freelance, services, consulting, sow
role: vendor
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Deliverables are accepted against agreed objective criteria. They count as accepted if the client doesn't reject them with written reasons within 5–10 business days. Payment is due 14–30 days from the invoice, not from "approval". For larger projects, a deposit of 30–50% up front.
**Fallback:** A 15-business-day review period. Milestone payments instead of a deposit.
**Walk-away:** Payment only on acceptance at the client's sole discretion, with no deadline.
**Red flags:** "acceptance at Client's sole discretion"; "to Client's satisfaction"; no deemed acceptance; payment "within 90 days after acceptance".
**Preferred language:** Client shall notify Contractor in writing within ten (10) business days of delivery of any material non-conformity with the agreed specification. Deliverables not rejected within that period are deemed accepted.

## PB-FL-002: Scope, revisions and change requests
applies_to: freelance, services, consulting, sow
role: vendor
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Scope defined in a SOW or proposal, with a set number of revision rounds (usually 2). Additional work is charged at our day or hour rate after written approval.
**Fallback:** 3 revision rounds.
**Walk-away:** "Unlimited revisions" or scope defined only as "all services Client may reasonably require".
**Red flags:** "unlimited revisions"; "including any related services"; fixed price with an open scope.
**Preferred language:** The Fees include up to two (2) rounds of revisions per Deliverable. Further changes will be charged at Contractor's then-current rates, subject to Client's prior written approval.

## PB-FL-003: Cancellation and kill fee
applies_to: freelance, services, consulting, sow
role: vendor
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** If the client cancels, they pay for work done to date plus a kill fee of 25–50% of the remaining fee for the current phase. Booked time cancelled at short notice is chargeable.
**Fallback:** Payment for work done plus non-cancellable expenses.
**Walk-away:** The client can cancel at any time without paying for work already done.
**Red flags:** "Client may terminate at any time without liability"; repayment of deposits on termination.
**Preferred language:** If Client terminates for convenience, Client shall pay for all work performed up to the termination date and a cancellation fee of twenty-five percent (25%) of the Fees for the then-current phase.

## PB-FL-004: Portfolio and credit
applies_to: freelance, services, consulting
role: vendor
priority: nice-to-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** We may show the final work in our portfolio after it is public, without revealing confidential information.
**Fallback:** Portfolio use after client approval, not unreasonably withheld.
**Walk-away:** Not applicable. Accept a ban if the work is genuinely confidential.
**Red flags:** Moral-rights waiver plus a ban on any reference to the project.
**Preferred language:** Contractor may display the Deliverables in its portfolio once they have been made public by Client, provided no Confidential Information is disclosed.

## PB-FL-005: Late payment
applies_to: freelance, services, consulting, sow
role: vendor
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Interest on overdue amounts at the statutory rate. We may pause work if payment is more than 14 days late, after a written warning. IP passes only on full payment.
**Fallback:** The right to pause work after 30 days.
**Walk-away:** No remedy for late payment combined with IP transferring before payment.
**Red flags:** IP assigned "upon creation"; set-off rights for the client; no interest.
**Preferred language:** Overdue amounts bear interest at the statutory rate. Contractor may suspend work if any invoice remains unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, following written notice.
