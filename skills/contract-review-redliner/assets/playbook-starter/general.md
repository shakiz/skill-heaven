# General terms

## PB-LAW-001: Governing law and jurisdiction
applies_to: all
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Our home law and courts. For cross-border deals, a neutral and well-known law (e.g. England and Wales) can be acceptable.
**Fallback:** The other party's law if it is a mainstream commercial jurisdiction and disputes go to courts or arbitration we can reasonably attend.
**Walk-away:** A jurisdiction where we could not realistically enforce our rights or defend a claim.
**Red flags:** Law and courts that don't match (e.g. New York law, courts in Singapore); mandatory arbitration with expensive rules for small deals; a jury-trial waiver or class-action waiver we didn't expect; governing law changed silently in a counter-draft.
**Preferred language:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of [our jurisdiction], and the courts of [our jurisdiction] have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.

## PB-ASSIGN-001: Assignment and change of control
applies_to: all
role: any
priority: nice-to-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** No assignment without consent (not unreasonably withheld), except to a group company or a buyer of the business. Subcontracting is allowed, but the subcontracting party stays responsible.
**Fallback:** Notice instead of consent for intra-group transfers.
**Walk-away:** The other party can assign to anyone, including our competitors, while we can't assign at all.
**Red flags:** One-sided assignment rights; termination on our change of control; "Supplier may assign or novate this Agreement at any time".
**Preferred language:** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party (not to be unreasonably withheld), except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

## PB-NONSOL-001: Non-solicitation and non-compete
applies_to: all
role: any
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** At most a mutual non-solicit of key staff directly involved in the work, lasting 6–12 months and allowing hiring through general job adverts. No non-compete restricting who we may work for or sell to.
**Fallback:** A 12-month non-solicit with a reasonable placement fee instead of a ban.
**Walk-away:** A non-compete covering our core business or all customers in an industry, or a non-solicit of customers.
**Red flags:** Non-competes hidden in NDAs, freelance contracts or reseller terms; "anywhere in the world"; durations over 12 months; restrictions covering our whole group.
**Preferred language:** During the term and for six (6) months after it, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was materially involved in the Services, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

## PB-PUB-001: Publicity and logo use
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, freelance, partnership
role: any
priority: nice-to-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** No use of the other party's name or logo without prior written consent, except (when we sell) listing the customer's name in a customer list.
**Fallback:** Name-only use in customer lists, with a right to withdraw consent.
**Walk-away:** Press releases or case studies about us without our approval.
**Red flags:** "Supplier may use Customer's name and logo in its marketing materials".
**Preferred language:** Neither party shall use the other party's name, logo or trade marks in publicity without the other party's prior written consent.

## PB-AUDIT-001: Audit rights
applies_to: msa, saas, services, supply, vendor, dpa, reseller, licence, license
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard (we are audited):** At most once a year, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, at the auditor's own cost, limited to records relating to this agreement, with confidentiality protections.
**Fallback (we are audited):** The auditor's costs move to us only if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than 5%.
**Walk-away (we are audited):** Unlimited on-site audits by a competitor-affiliated auditor, or licence-audit fees at list price with no negotiation.
**Red flags:** Audits "at any time"; audit costs always borne by the audited party; back-payment of licence fees at undiscounted list price.
**Preferred language:** Audits may take place no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, during normal business hours, and at the auditing party's cost.

## PB-EXCL-001: Exclusivity and minimum commitments
applies_to: msa, supply, vendor, reseller, partnership, services
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** No exclusivity and no minimum purchase or volume commitments, unless we priced them in deliberately.
**Fallback:** Exclusivity limited to a narrow product or territory, for a short term, in return for a clear commercial benefit.
**Walk-away:** Exclusivity across our whole business, or take-or-pay commitments we can't forecast.
**Red flags:** "exclusive supplier"; "Customer shall purchase a minimum of"; shortfall payments; most-favoured-nation clauses.
**Preferred language:** Nothing in this Agreement prevents either party from entering into similar arrangements with third parties.
