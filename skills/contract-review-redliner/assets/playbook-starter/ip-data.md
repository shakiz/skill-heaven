# Intellectual property and data

## PB-IP-001: Ownership of deliverables (we buy)
applies_to: services, consulting, sow, freelance, msa
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** We own the bespoke deliverables we pay for, with ownership passing on payment. We get a perpetual, royalty-free licence to any supplier background IP included in them, so we can use and change the deliverables.
**Fallback:** The supplier keeps ownership but grants an exclusive (or perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive) licence covering everything we need, including the right to modify and sublicense to our group and contractors.
**Walk-away:** Only a revocable or term-limited licence to deliverables we paid to have built.
**Red flags:** "All Deliverables remain the property of Supplier"; licences that end when the agreement ends; no right to modify or use other contractors.
**Preferred language:** Upon payment of the relevant Fees, all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables (excluding Supplier's Background IP) shall vest in Customer. Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use any Background IP incorporated in the Deliverables as necessary to use and modify them.

## PB-IP-002: Pre-existing IP and tools (we sell)
applies_to: services, consulting, sow, freelance, msa
role: vendor
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** We keep everything we owned before the project and anything general we develop: tools, code libraries, templates, know-how. The customer owns (or gets a licence to) the deliverables made specifically for them, and only once they have paid.
**Fallback:** Assign bespoke deliverables on payment. Give a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to background IP embedded in them.
**Walk-away:** Assigning our pre-existing IP, tools or general know-how, or IP transferring before payment.
**Red flags:** "all work product ... including pre-existing materials"; "whether or not created for Client"; moral-rights waivers combined with no portfolio right; assignment of "ideas, concepts and know-how".
**Preferred language:** Contractor retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how ("Background IP"). Subject to full payment, Contractor assigns to Client the intellectual property rights in the Deliverables created specifically for Client and grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use the Background IP to the extent incorporated in the Deliverables.

## PB-IP-003: Customer data use, feedback and AI training
applies_to: saas, msa, services, dpa
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** The supplier uses our data only to provide the services to us. Anonymised, aggregated statistics are acceptable. Using our data or content to train AI models needs our prior written opt-in.
**Fallback:** Use of aggregated, de-identified usage metrics for product improvement. No training on our content.
**Walk-away:** Rights to use, copy or commercialise our data or content for any purpose, or to train models on it.
**Red flags:** "Supplier may use Customer Data to improve its products and services"; "perpetual, irrevocable licence to Customer Data"; "machine learning"; "aggregate data" that isn't anonymised.
**Preferred language:** Supplier shall process Customer Data solely to provide the Services to Customer. Supplier shall not use Customer Data or Customer content to train, develop or improve any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model without Customer's prior written consent.

## PB-CONF-001: Confidentiality in commercial contracts
applies_to: msa, saas, services, consulting, sow, supply, vendor, freelance, partnership
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Mutual obligations with the usual exceptions (already public, already known, independently developed, received from a third party, required by law). They last for the term plus 3–5 years, and indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data.
**Fallback:** 2 years after the term for ordinary business information.
**Walk-away:** One-way confidentiality that protects only the other side when we also share sensitive information.
**Red flags:** No exceptions; perpetual obligations for all information; one-way obligations; no right to share with advisers and subcontractors who need to know.
**Preferred language:** Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement, during the term and for five (5) years after termination (and, for trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets).

## PB-DATA-001: Data protection
applies_to: saas, msa, services, dpa, consulting, sow
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Where the supplier processes personal data for us, a DPA meets the applicable law (e.g. GDPR Art. 28): instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processor approval with notice and a right to object, breach notice within 48 hours, help with data-subject requests, audit, deletion or return at the end, and a lawful mechanism for international transfers.
**Fallback:** Breach notice "without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours"; a general authorisation for listed sub-processors with 30 days' notice of changes.
**Walk-away:** No DPA while personal data is processed, or transfers outside the permitted regions with no safeguards.
**Red flags:** The supplier described as an independent controller of our customer data; "Supplier may engage sub-processors at its discretion"; breach notice "promptly" with no time limit; DPA liability excluded or tiny.
**Preferred language:** Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

## PB-DATA-002: Data return and deletion at exit
applies_to: saas, msa, services, dpa
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** On termination we can export all our data in a usable, standard format for at least 30 days at no extra cost. After that the supplier deletes it and confirms in writing, except where the law requires it to be kept.
**Fallback:** 14 days of export access; reasonable fees for assisted migration.
**Walk-away:** Data deleted immediately on termination, or export only for a fee set by the supplier.
**Red flags:** No exit clause at all; "Supplier may delete Customer Data upon termination"; proprietary export formats.
**Preferred language:** For thirty (30) days after termination, Supplier shall make Customer Data available for export in a commonly used machine-readable format at no additional charge, and shall then delete it and certify deletion in writing, save as required by law.

## PB-SEC-001: Information security
applies_to: saas, msa, services, dpa
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Appropriate technical and organisational measures, described in a security schedule or by certification (ISO 27001 or SOC 2), kept at least as protective during the term. We may review recent reports or certificates.
**Fallback:** The supplier's published security documentation, provided it cannot be materially reduced.
**Walk-away:** No security commitments at all when the supplier hosts our confidential or personal data.
**Red flags:** "commercially reasonable efforts" as the only security commitment; security terms that can change at will.
**Preferred language:** Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures that are no less protective than those described in Schedule [X] throughout the term.
