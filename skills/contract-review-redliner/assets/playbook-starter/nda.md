# Non-disclosure agreements

## PB-NDA-001: Definition of Confidential Information
applies_to: nda
role: recipient
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Information marked confidential, or information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, disclosed for the stated purpose. The standard exceptions apply: public, already known, independently developed, received from a third party, required by law.
**Fallback:** All information disclosed "in connection with the Purpose", as long as the standard exceptions stay.
**Walk-away:** Definitions without exceptions that would block our ordinary business.
**Red flags:** "all information of any kind, whether or not marked, disclosed before or after the date of this Agreement"; no independent-development exception; the existence of discussions treated as confidential forever.
**Preferred language:** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

## PB-NDA-002: Mutuality
applies_to: nda
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** Mutual obligations when both sides will share information. A one-way NDA is acceptable only when only one side discloses, and only if we are that side or the information we receive is genuinely limited.
**Fallback:** One-way in their favour, if we disclose nothing sensitive.
**Walk-away:** One-way in their favour while we are expected to share sensitive material (pricing, roadmaps, code).
**Red flags:** A title that says "Mutual" but obligations that run only one way; definitions of "Disclosing Party" that only ever mean them.
**Preferred language:** Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and each party's obligations under this Agreement apply to Confidential Information it receives.

## PB-NDA-003: Term and survival
applies_to: nda
role: recipient
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** A disclosure period of 1–2 years, with obligations surviving 2–5 years after disclosure, and trade secrets protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.
**Fallback:** Obligations last 5 years.
**Walk-away:** Perpetual obligations for all information, when we are the recipient of broad information.
**Red flags:** "in perpetuity"; "shall survive indefinitely"; no end date for the agreement at all.
**Preferred language:** The Recipient's obligations shall continue for three (3) years from the date of each disclosure, save that obligations in respect of trade secrets shall continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret.

## PB-NDA-004: Residuals clause
applies_to: nda
role: discloser
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** No residuals clause when we are the one disclosing.
**Fallback:** Residuals limited to general skills and know-how, expressly excluding source code, pricing, customer data, patents and trade secrets.
**Walk-away:** A residuals clause covering anything kept in unaided memory, including our specific technical or commercial information.
**Red flags:** "Recipient may use Residuals for any purpose"; "information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel".
**Preferred language:** (Delete the residuals clause.)

## PB-NDA-005: Compelled disclosure, return and remedies
applies_to: nda
role: any
priority: important
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** If disclosure is required by law, the recipient gives prompt notice where legally allowed and discloses only what is required. On request, information is returned or destroyed, except backup copies and copies the law requires keeping, which stay confidential. Injunctive relief is available. No licence is granted, and there is no obligation to proceed with any deal.
**Fallback:** Certification of destruction by email.
**Walk-away:** Penalty or liquidated-damages clauses for any breach.
**Red flags:** Liquidated damages; "Recipient shall indemnify Discloser for any breach"; destruction obligations that ignore backups; an obligation to enter into a transaction.
**Preferred language:** On written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Confidential Information, except that it may retain copies in automatic electronic backups or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## PB-NDA-006: Hidden obligations in NDAs
applies_to: nda
role: any
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: starter
owner: set an owner

**Standard:** An NDA covers confidentiality only. Non-solicits, non-competes, standstills, exclusivity, IP assignments and feedback licences belong in other agreements, if anywhere.
**Fallback:** A mutual non-solicit of staff directly involved, for 6–12 months, with a general-advertising exception.
**Walk-away:** A non-compete, exclusivity, a standstill (unless this is an M&A deal and a lawyer has reviewed it) or an IP assignment inside an NDA.
**Red flags:** "shall not ... compete"; "shall not solicit any customer"; "exclusive negotiation"; "all feedback and improvements shall vest in Discloser".
**Preferred language:** (Delete obligations unrelated to confidentiality.)
