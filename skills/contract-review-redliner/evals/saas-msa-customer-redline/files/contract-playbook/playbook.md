# Northwind Digital contract playbook (we mostly BUY software and services)

## PB-LIAB-001: Limitation of liability
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CFO

**Standard:** Mutual cap of the fees paid and payable in the 12 months before the claim. Supplier's cap must not apply to data protection or confidentiality breaches.
**Fallback:** We will accept a cap of 18 months' fees for us if the supplier's cap rises to the same level; super-cap of NOK 2 million for data breaches.
**Walk-away:** Any uncapped liability for Northwind.
**Red flags:** caps for one party only; fees "paid" in 3 months.
**Preferred language:** Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid and payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## PB-RENEW-001: Renewal and notice
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CFO

**Standard:** Renewal for 12-month periods at most, non-renewal notice of no more than 60 days.
**Fallback:** 90 days' notice is tolerable if renewal periods are 12 months; the board budget cycle lets us live with it.
**Walk-away:** Renewal for multi-year periods.
**Preferred language:** This Agreement shall renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

## PB-PRICE-001: Price increases
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CFO

**Standard:** No increases in the Initial Term; afterwards at most once a year on 90 days' notice, capped at CPI.
**Fallback:** CPI plus 3 percent, because procurement has budgeted 6% annual uplift for SaaS tools.
**Walk-away:** Uncapped increases at any time.

## PB-DATA-001: Customer data use and AI training
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CTO

**Standard:** Supplier may use Customer Data only to provide the Services to us. No AI/ML training on our data.
**Fallback:** Aggregated anonymised usage statistics only.
**Walk-away:** Any training of models on Customer Data.

## PB-DATA-002: Data export and deletion at exit
applies_to: saas, msa
role: customer
priority: must-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CTO

**Standard:** 30 days to export all Customer Data in a standard format at no cost, then deletion with written confirmation.
**Fallback:** 14 days.
**Walk-away:** Immediate deletion on termination.

## PB-SLA-001: Service levels
applies_to: saas
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: CTO

**Standard:** 99.5% monthly availability; credits do not exclude other remedies; termination right for repeated failure.
**Fallback:** 99.0% if credits are not the sole remedy.
**Walk-away:** None (not a deal-breaker on its own).

## PB-AMEND-001: Online policies and unilateral changes
applies_to: saas, msa
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: Legal

**Standard:** Referenced policies fixed at signature; changes may not materially reduce our rights.
**Fallback:** Changes on 30 days' notice with termination right.
**Walk-away:** Supplier can change terms at will.

## PB-TERM-001: Termination
applies_to: saas, msa, services
role: customer
priority: important
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: Legal

**Standard:** No supplier termination for convenience during the Initial Term; mutual termination for breach with 30-day cure.
**Fallback:** Supplier convenience termination on 12 months' notice with pro-rata refund.
**Walk-away:** Supplier can walk away on short notice while we are locked in for 36 months.

## PB-LAW-001: Governing law
applies_to: all
role: any
priority: nice-to-have
last_reviewed: 2026-06-01
owner: Legal

**Standard:** Norwegian law, Oslo courts.
**Fallback:** English law and courts are acceptable.
**Walk-away:** US state law with US courts.
