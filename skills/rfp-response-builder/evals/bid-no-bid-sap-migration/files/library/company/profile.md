## CO-001: Company overview
tags: company, profile, about, size, employees, history, founded
last_reviewed: 2026-08-01
owner: CEO
approved_for: external

Northwind Digital ApS is a software development and digital services company founded in 2011. We have 140 employees (October 2026): 95 developers, 14 UX/UI designers, 12 QA engineers, 10 project and delivery managers and 9 in sales and administration. Offices: Copenhagen (headquarters, 60 staff) and Kraków (80 staff). We build web and mobile platforms for public-sector bodies, insurance and logistics companies in the Nordics.
