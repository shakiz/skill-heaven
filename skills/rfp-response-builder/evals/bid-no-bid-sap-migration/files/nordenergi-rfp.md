# Invitation to Tender: SAP S/4HANA Migration and Finance Transformation

**Buyer:** NordEnergi A/S (national electricity distribution company, 2,400 employees)
**Reference:** NE-ITT-2026-08
**Deadline for questions:** 20 November 2026
**Submission deadline:** 10 December 2026

## 1. Scope

NordEnergi will migrate from SAP ECC 6.0 to SAP S/4HANA (brownfield conversion) including finance,
procurement, asset management (SAP PM) and integration with SCADA and metering systems. Estimated
value: EUR 18-22 million over 3 years. Go-live required by Q1 2028.

## 2. Qualification requirements (all mandatory)

2.1 Minimum annual turnover of EUR 50 million in each of the last three financial years.
2.2 The supplier must be an SAP Gold Partner (or SAP Partner Edge "Gold" level) with S/4HANA specialisation.
2.3 At least three completed S/4HANA conversion projects for utilities or energy companies in the last five years.
2.4 A team of at least 25 SAP-certified consultants available from April 2027.
2.5 ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
2.6 Security-cleared personnel for work on critical infrastructure (NIS2 scope).

## 3. Evaluation

Price 40%, quality of solution 35%, experience 25%.
