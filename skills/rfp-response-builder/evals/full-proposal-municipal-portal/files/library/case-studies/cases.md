# Case studies

## CS-001: Aarvik Municipality - citizen self-service portal
tags: public sector, municipality, citizen portal, self-service, accessibility, wcag, case study, reference
last_reviewed: 2026-02-01
owner: Account manager public sector
approved_for: external

Client: Aarvik Municipality (Denmark, 85,000 residents). Period: March 2023 - June 2024. Problem: citizens had to call or visit to apply for 40+ services; the call centre was overloaded. Solution: a self-service portal with MitID login, digital forms for 42 services, case status tracking and integration with the municipality's case management system. Built WCAG 2.1 AA. Results: 38% fewer calls to the call centre within 6 months of launch; 71% of applications submitted digitally after one year. Tech: .NET, React, Azure. Reference: yes - Head of Digitalisation, contact details on request.
