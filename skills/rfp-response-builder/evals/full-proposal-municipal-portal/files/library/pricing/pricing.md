## PRC-002: Pricing models and approval rules
tags: pricing, fixed price, time and materials, discount, approval, risk buffer
last_reviewed: 2026-01-10
owner: CFO
approved_for: internal-only

Internal: fixed-price bids must include a risk buffer of at least 15% on the estimate. Discounts up to 10% can be approved by the sales director; above 10% requires CEO approval. Never quote below the rate card without approval.
