# Request for Proposal: Vestby Digital Citizen Portal

**Contracting authority:** Vestby Municipality (Denmark, 52,000 residents)
**Reference:** VM-2026-031
**Procedure:** Open tender
**Submission deadline:** 4 December 2026, 12:00 CET, via the Vestby e-tender portal
**Format:** One PDF, maximum 25 pages excluding CVs and appendices, in English or Danish.

## 1. Background

Vestby Municipality receives about 120,000 citizen phone calls a year. Most concern applications for
services such as parking permits, waste collection, child care places and building permits. Today most
applications are paper or PDF forms sent by email. The municipality wants a citizen portal where residents
can log in with MitID, apply for services, follow the status of their cases and receive messages.

Goals: (1) reduce call volume by 30% within two years; (2) at least 60% of applications submitted digitally;
(3) full compliance with the Danish web accessibility act (WCAG 2.1 AA).

## 2. Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Solution and delivery approach | 40% |
| Price | 35% |
| Relevant experience and team | 25% |

## 3. Mandatory requirements (pass/fail)

3.1 The supplier **must** hold a valid ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
3.2 The supplier **must** have professional liability insurance of at least EUR 5 million.
3.3 The solution **must** comply with WCAG 2.1 level AA.
3.4 All personal data **must** be stored and processed within the EU/EEA.
3.5 The supplier **must** provide three references from public-sector projects completed within the last five years, with contact persons.

## 4. Questions to answer

4.1 Describe your understanding of Vestby's needs and your proposed solution. (max 600 words)
4.2 Describe your development methodology and how the municipality will be involved. (max 400 words)
4.3 Describe how you ensure accessibility throughout the project. (max 300 words)
4.4 Describe your approach to information security and GDPR, including incident notification. (max 400 words)
4.5 Present the key personnel you propose, with their relevant experience. Attach CVs.
4.6 Describe your support and maintenance offering. The municipality requires support 24/7 for critical incidents with a response time of 30 minutes.
4.7 The supplier's project manager and lead developer must be on-site at Vestby town hall at least two days per week during the build phase. Confirm and describe how you will meet this.
4.8 Describe your approach to testing and quality assurance. (max 300 words)
4.9 Describe how the municipality will own the source code and how handover to another supplier would work.

## 5. Price

5.1 Provide a fixed price for discovery and build (estimated 9 months), and hourly rates for change requests.
5.2 Provide a monthly price for support and maintenance including the 24/7 critical-incident coverage in 4.6.
