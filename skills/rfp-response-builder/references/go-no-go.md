# Bid / no-bid assessment

Writing a proposal costs real senior time, often days. The best way to raise the win rate is to bid on fewer, better-fitting opportunities. Run this check before drafting.

## 1. Knock-out checks (any "no" means no-bid unless the user can resolve it)

- Can we meet every **mandatory / pass-fail** requirement? Examples: a certification, minimum turnover, insurance level, security clearance, local presence, language.
- Can we submit on time in the required format?
- Is there any conflict of interest or legal or ethical issue?

List each knock-out with evidence from the RFP and the library. If the library can't confirm it, it's "unknown" and must be asked about, not assumed.

## 2. Weighted score (0–100)

Score each factor 0–5 from evidence. Multiply by the weight and sum. Then score = total / 5.

| Factor | Weight | 5 looks like | 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capability fit | 25 | Core service; several similar case studies in the library | Outside our offering |
| Relevant references | 15 | 3+ comparable, referenceable projects | None |
| Client relationship | 15 | Existing client or warm contact; we shaped the requirements | Unknown client, no contact |
| Competitive position | 15 | Clear differentiator that matches the evaluation criteria | Incumbent strongly favored, or the RFP is written for a rival |
| Commercial attractiveness | 15 | Healthy size and margin; strategic account or sector | Low value, or price-only evaluation below our rates |
| Delivery capacity & risk | 15 | Team available; realistic scope and timeline | No capacity; unrealistic terms or liabilities |

Where you lack information (relationship, competitors), score 2–3, mark it "assumed", and say what would change the score.

## 3. Recommendation

- **75+ → Go**: bid with full effort.
- **50–74 → Conditional go**: bid if the named conditions are met (e.g. "confirm architect availability", "partner for the SAP part").
- **< 50, or any unresolved knock-out → No-bid**: recommend declining, or ask a clarifying question to the buyer before the Q&A deadline.

## Output (bid-assessment.md)

1. Recommendation and score in one line.
2. A knock-out table: check · result · evidence.
3. A score table: factor · score · rationale.
4. Top 3 risks and top 3 win themes, if bidding.
5. Questions to send to the buyer during the clarification window, if any.
